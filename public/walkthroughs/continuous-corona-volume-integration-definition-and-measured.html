Scope: Current initial-attachment implementation; clean tracked source

The production continuous method computes polarity-selected partial tetrahedron volumes and multiplies them by positive radial-integration weights. It removes the whole-cell on/off approximation in volume_integral. B. Which errors continuous integration removes—and keeps

volume_integral evaluates membership once at each cell centroid. An intersected cell therefore contributes either nothing or its entire approximate radial flux. Continuous integration replaces that binary decision with a partial-volume fraction.
